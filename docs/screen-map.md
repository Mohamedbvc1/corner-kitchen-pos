# Corner Kitchen - Screen Map

Checkpoint 1 - November 16, 2026

## Customer

Sign in / Create account
        |
        v
       Menu
        |
        v
      Cart / Checkout
        |
        v
     Order status
        |
        v
      Picked up

- Forgot password is available on the sign-in page.
- Customers can browse items and add available items to the cart.
- Checkout shows the total and uses simulated payment.
- Order status shows Placed, Preparing, Ready, and Picked up.

## Staff

Sign in
   |
   v
Orders board
   |-- Menu items
   |-- Inventory
   |-- Sales report

- The Orders board moves an order from New to Preparing to Ready, then Picked up.
- Menu items lets staff add, edit, show, or hide an item.
- Inventory shows the number of cans and bottles and lets staff restock.
- Sales report shows daily paid orders and totals.
